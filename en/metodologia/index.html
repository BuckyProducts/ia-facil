Ranking methodology

Each tool gets 1 to 5 points on five criteria. The final score is a weighted average.

Criteria and weights
CriterionWeightHow it is measured
Quality30%Output quality on typical tasks for the category (editorial assessment).
Free tier25%How much you can do without paying: daily limits, credits, watermarks.
Spanish20%Spanish: yes = 5 (Spanish UI and output), partial = 3, unverified = 1.
Price10%Entry paid plan price on the official site (USD/month): ≤10 = 5, ≤20 = 4, ≤30 = 3, ≤60 = 2, more = 1. Free = 5. If we could not verify the price, neutral score 3.
Reliability15%Reliability: product maturity, stability and company backing (editorial assessment).

Tiers

  • S ≥ 4.4
  • A 3.9–4.39
  • B 3.4–3.89
  • C 2.9–3.39
  • D < 2.9

Honesty and limits

  • Quality and reliability are Bucky Products editorial judgments; price, free tier and language are based on official pages checked on 2026-10-15.
  • Sponsorships never change a score or tier.
  • Prices change often: always confirm on the official site. Some prices are the monthly equivalent of the annual plan.
  • Spot a mistake? Tell us and we will fix it in the next review.
